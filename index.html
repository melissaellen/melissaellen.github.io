02 — About

Hi, I'm Melissa — I design with creativity and ship with confidence.

I'm a front-end developer with a background in design and a deep love for research. I believe the best design is invisible — it just feels right. I specialize in discovery and making complicated things feel simple.

When I'm not pushing pixels in Figma, I'm running user interviews, facilitating design sprints, or convincing developers that just a few more pixels of breathing room really does matter.
